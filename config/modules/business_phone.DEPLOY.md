# Business phone number (external service)

Catalog-only third-party service — not a REΛVE plugin. Invoice and bookkeeping only.

A dedicated business number so the owner stops handing out a personal cell. The carrier bills the monthly line separately. Do not enable in install `features[]`. Must not appear on deploy, the demo builder, or the public add-ons page.

## External setup

- Provision a local or toll-free number
- Forwarding / voicemail as agreed
- Invoice REΛVE setup separately from the carrier
