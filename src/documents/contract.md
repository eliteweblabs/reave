# Web Design Service Agreement

Prepared for **{client.name}**{client.company_str} · **{date}**

## 1. Parties

This Web Design Service Agreement ("**Agreement**") is entered into as of **{date}**
between **{company.legal_name}** ("**Company**") and **{client.name}**{client.company_str}
("**Client**").

Client contact: **{client.email}** · **{client.phone}**

## 2. Scope of Work

Company agrees to provide web design, development, and related digital services as mutually agreed upon in writing
(email or messenger) prior to the start of each project phase. All scope changes require written confirmation
before work begins.

## 3. Payment Terms

A deposit of **50%** of the agreed project total is due before work begins. The remaining balance
is due upon project completion and before final delivery of files or site launch, whichever comes first.
Invoices unpaid after **14 days** may incur a **1.5% monthly late fee**.

## 4. Revisions

Each project phase includes up to **two (2) rounds of revisions**. Additional revision rounds
are billed at the standard hourly rate. A revision is defined as changes to work already presented and
approved — new feature requests constitute additional scope.

## 5. Intellectual Property

Upon receipt of full payment, Client receives full ownership of all custom-created design assets and code
specific to their project. Company retains the right to display the finished work in its portfolio.
Third-party assets (stock photos, fonts, plugins) remain subject to their respective licenses.

## 6. Client Responsibilities

Client **{client.name}** is responsible for providing all content (text, images, logos) in a
timely manner. Delays caused by Client may push project timelines and do not entitle Client to a refund of
any deposit paid.

## 7. Limitation of Liability

Company's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total amount paid by Client in the
three (3) months preceding the claim. Company is not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential
damages of any kind.

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement with **7 days' written notice**. Upon termination,
Company will invoice for all work completed to date. Deposits are non-refundable unless Company fails
to begin work within an agreed timeframe.

## 9. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the jurisdiction in which Company operates, effective
**{year}**. Any disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration before litigation.

## 10. Entire Agreement

This document constitutes the entire agreement between **{company.legal_name}** and
**{client.name}** and supersedes all prior discussions. Modifications must be in writing
and signed by both parties.
