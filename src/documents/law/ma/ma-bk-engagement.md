## 2. Scope

Counsel will advise Client on chapter selection, prepare and file the petition and schedules Client timely completes, appear at the meeting of creditors, and handle routine administration of a **Chapter 7 or Chapter 13** case in the division that matches Client’s county.

This engagement does not include adversary proceedings, appeals, stay-relief contests, or non-bankruptcy work unless we sign a separate writing.

## 3. Fees

The flat fee and filing-fee advance for this matter are as we discussed and will be restated on the signed fee agreement. Unpaid balances may delay filing. Additional work outside § 2 is billed only with Client’s written approval.

## 4. Client duties

Client will give complete, accurate information; deliver the documents we request; complete an approved credit-counseling briefing before filing; and tell Counsel immediately about new lawsuits, levies, transfers, or income changes.

## 5. No guarantee

Counsel cannot promise a discharge, a particular trustee or judge, or that every debt will be listed or eliminated. Outcomes depend on the facts, the Code, and the court.

## 6. Governing law

This engagement is governed by Massachusetts law and the United States Bankruptcy Code. Venue for the case is the District of Massachusetts.
