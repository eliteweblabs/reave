## Why this matters before a Massachusetts filing

Massachusetts allows a recorded declaration of homestead under MGL c. 188. In many consumer cases Counsel recommends recording **before** the petition so the homestead is on record when exemptions are claimed. You may later elect Massachusetts or federal exemptions (11 U.S.C. § 522); that choice is made on the petition, not in this letter.

This letter does not file anything with the Bankruptcy Court.

## What we need from you

- Current deed or recorded ownership for the home you occupy
- Names of every owner on title (including a spouse who is not filing)
- The registry district (Essex South, Middlesex North, Suffolk, etc.)
- Confirmation nobody else already recorded a homestead on this property

## Next step

Once we have the deed, Counsel will prepare a declaration for your signature and tell you where to record it. Recording fees are paid to the registry, not as a court filing fee.

If the house is in a manufactured-housing community or held in a trust, say so — the form and registry path change.
