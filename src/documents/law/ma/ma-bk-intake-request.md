**{company.name}** to **{client.name}** · **{date}**

Please send the following so we can prepare your District of Massachusetts petition. Reply-all to **{company.support_email}** or upload to the matter if we have already opened one.

## Identity

- Government photo ID and Social Security card (or official SSA printout)
- Prior names, DBAs, and addresses for the last three years
- Spouse / joint-debtor information, if anyone is filing with you
- City and county of your current home (this picks Boston, Worcester, or Springfield)

## Income and taxes

- Pay stubs for the last 60 days, or a signed no-income statement
- Federal and Massachusetts tax returns for the last two years
- Proof of any benefits, support, or retirement income

## Accounts and property

- Bank statements for the last 3–6 months (all accounts)
- Vehicle titles and loan statements; mortgage / HELOC statements
- Retirement and brokerage statements
- Lawsuits, garnishments, foreclosure, or tax-taking notices

## Before we can file

- Credit-counseling certificate from an approved agency (it expires)
- Signed engagement and fee agreement

We cannot file until counseling is current and the petition information is complete. Call **{company.name}** if gathering any item will take more than a week.
