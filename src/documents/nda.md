# Non-Disclosure Agreement

Between **{company.name}** and **{client.name}**{client.company_str} · **{date}**

## 1. Parties

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("**Agreement**") is made as of **{date}** between
**{company.legal_name}** ("**Disclosing Party**") and **{client.name}**{client.company_str}
("**Receiving Party**"). Together referred to as the "Parties."

Receiving Party contact: **{client.email}**

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

"**Confidential Information**" means any non-public information disclosed by Disclosing Party
to **{client.first_name}**, including but not limited to: business strategies, pricing,
client lists, source code, design files, trade secrets, and any information marked as confidential or
that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the context of disclosure.

## 3. Obligations of the Receiving Party

**{client.name}** agrees to:

- Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence.
- Not disclose any Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent.
- Use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of evaluating or engaging in a business
  relationship with Disclosing Party.
- Limit internal access to Confidential Information on a need-to-know basis.

## 4. Exclusions

These obligations do not apply to information that:

- Is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement by **{client.name}**.
- Was already known to Receiving Party prior to disclosure.
- Is independently developed by **{client.name}** without use of Confidential Information.
- Is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order (with prompt notice to Disclosing Party).

## 5. Term

This Agreement shall remain in effect for a period of **three (3) years** from
**{date}**, unless earlier terminated in writing by both Parties.

## 6. Return of Information

Upon request or termination, **{client.name}** shall promptly return or certify destruction
of all materials containing Confidential Information.

## 7. No License

Nothing in this Agreement grants **{client.name}** any rights, title, or license in or to
any Confidential Information, intellectual property, or technology of Disclosing Party.

## 8. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the jurisdiction in which
Disclosing Party operates, effective **{year}**. Breach of this Agreement may result in
irreparable harm for which monetary damages may be inadequate, entitling Disclosing Party to seek
injunctive relief.
