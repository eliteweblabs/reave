# Consumer bankruptcy intake checklist

Operations list for a new debtor matter. The attorney decides chapter and strategy.

## Identity and conflicts

- Full legal name, prior names, DBA
- Spouse / joint debtor, and whether they are filing
- City and county — that picks the division (`list_nearby_courts`)
- SSN last four; prior filings (dates, chapter, district)
- Conflict check: creditors, landlords, ex-spouses, partners

## Documents to collect early

- Last 60 days of pay stubs (or a no-income affidavit)
- Last two years of federal and state tax returns
- Bank statements (typically 3–6 months)
- Vehicle titles / loan statements; mortgage / HELOC
- Credit counseling certificate (approved agency; expires)
- Collection suits, garnishments, foreclosure or tax-taking notices

## After retainers

1. Open a matter `{Client} — Chapter {7|13|11}`.
2. Put counseling, petition target, and a placeholder 341 on Todos.
3. When the case is assigned, write judge, case number, trustee, and 341 Zoom on the matter from the notice — cross-check `courts-in-radius`.
