# Bankruptcy department — District of Massachusetts

Compiled Aug 2026. Bankruptcy is federal. **Which division a client uses depends on county** — and a few Essex / Middlesex / Norfolk towns are carved out. Always check `courts-in-radius` for the office pin + gate, then the case notice for judge and trustee.

## Court structure

Three divisions of the U.S. Bankruptcy Court, District of Massachusetts. Website [mab.uscourts.gov](https://www.mab.uscourts.gov) · CM/ECF [ecf.mab.uscourts.gov](https://ecf.mab.uscourts.gov) · calendar [ecf.mab.uscourts.gov/cgi-bin/hearings.pl](https://ecf.mab.uscourts.gov/cgi-bin/hearings.pl)

- **Eastern (Boston)** — most of Essex, Suffolk, listed Middlesex / Norfolk towns, Plymouth, Bristol, Barnstable, Nantucket, Dukes. McCormack, 5 Post Office Square, Suite 1150.
- **Central (Worcester)** — Worcester County, plus Essex towns Andover, Bradford, Haverhill, Lawrence, Methuen, North Andover.
- **Western (Springfield)** — Berkshire, Franklin, Hampden, Hampshire.

Phones, hours, and chambers for venues **inside this office’s gate** are in `courts-in-radius`. Do not guess a number that is not in that book.

## Massachusetts-specific

- MGL c. 235, § 34 — state exemptions
- Homestead Act, MGL c. 188 — recording before filing is often recommended
- Debtors may elect state or federal exemptions (11 U.S.C. § 522). Federal amounts adjust periodically (last set Apr 1, 2025–Mar 31, 2028)
- MA DOR Bankruptcy Unit: [mass.gov/service-details/bankruptcy](https://www.mass.gov/service-details/bankruptcy)
