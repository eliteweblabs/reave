Services

Looking for pet services?

From daycare and training to boarding, grooming, and supplies — owned and managed by experienced trainers. No overcrowding. No aversives.

Dog daycare

Climate-controlled indoor space, outdoor play, and separate areas for small and large dogs. Target 1:7 staff-to-dog ratio with daily enrichment.

Dog grooming

Force-free baths and haircuts. We work with you so your dog comes out looking how you hoped — and still wants to come back.

Walking & sitting

Walks in Beverly, Salem, Hamilton, and Wenham. Overnight sitting in a staff home, or visits to yours — cats welcome too.

Training

Positive reinforcement and science-based methods only. No spray bottles. No citronella. Just dogs learning in a way that sticks.

Boarding

Keep the routine. Dogs stay with staff or we come to your house so your pet can remain at home while you’re away.

Pet supplies

Healthy toys, treats, kibble, frozen raw, freeze-dried, and enrichment — the shop is here when you pick up.
